My favorite hobby is the sport I do here, Acrobatics & Tumbling.

I enjoy this hobby because I find it very fun. I love that I get to hang out with nice people and try cool tricks. It keeps me active and on a good schedule.

I got into this hobby during my second semester here. I did cheerleading in high school and knew this was similar, so I decided to give it a try. The coach reached out to me and I was nervous at first, but I thought it might be fun.

  • Compulsory Acro is my favorite event. It makes me the most anxious, but it's also the most rewarding.
  • Pyramids are another one of my favorites.
  • Team Routine is the most upbeat and it's very fun to work together as a whole team.
  1. Before you start, you need to warm-up. We have a warm-up and stretching sequence that we perform before each practice.
  2. You need to use the weight room. You have three set workouts in the gym per week.
  3. You need to be a good teammate. You have to work together and lift each other up, physically and emotionally.
